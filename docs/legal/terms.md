Hasala is an experimental beta. It is available by invite only, through Apple's TestFlight. It is not a finished product.

- Features change often, sometimes without notice.
- Things can break, and your data can be lost or reset.
- Don't rely on Hasala as your only record of your money. Your bank's statements are the record.

Please use Hasala with that in mind. [The beta](#the-beta), below, says more.

If you want a copy of your own, export your data from time to time, from Profile › Settings › Export your data.

Hasala is English only. It runs on iPhone, and reads bank alerts from a Gmail account or a personal Microsoft Outlook account (such as outlook.com, hotmail.com or live.com). At the time of writing, it supports alerts from Dubai Islamic Bank (DIB), Emirates NBD and Abu Dhabi Islamic Bank (ADIB).

## Not financial advice

Hasala helps you see and plan your own spending. It is not a bank, a payment service or a financial adviser.

- Nothing in Hasala is financial, investment, tax or legal advice.
- Budgets, pace estimates and bill reminders are calculated automatically from your own data. Categories come from your rules, a built-in merchant list, your own earlier picks and, if you turn them on, Cloud suggestions (with Apple's on-device model when Cloudflare can't help). All of these, including any transaction that an Always use rule or Sort automatically files for you, can be wrong or incomplete.
- Hasala does not move money, make payments, or connect to your bank account. It reads the alert emails your bank sends you.
- Your bank's own statements are the record of your money. Don't rely on Hasala as your only record, and check important figures against your statements before you rely on them.

## Your account

### Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old and able to form a binding contract to use Hasala. Hasala is meant for people managing their own personal finances in the UAE.

### Your account details

You agree to give a real email address that you control and keep it up to date. Your display name can be anything that isn't offensive or misleading.

### Keeping it safe

You are responsible for keeping your sign-in methods and your iPhone secure. In particular:

- **Your 12-word recovery phrase is your account.** Anyone who has it can read your data, sign in as you and add devices. Keep it private and stored somewhere safe.
- **We can't reset your recovery phrase.** If you lose your recovery phrase and every passkey, and have no signed-in iPhone, your account and data can't be recovered. Not even by us.
- **Password reset is not in the beta.** If you can't sign in, the way back is your 12 words with a code we email you. If you sign in with email and password, keep your 12 words safe.
- **Hasala will never ask for your recovery phrase, your password or a code** in an email, a notification, a phone call or on a website. Only type them into the Hasala app.
- Keep a passcode on your iPhone, and keep two-factor authentication on for your Apple Account. Your passkey is stored in iCloud Keychain.

Tell us straight away at security@hasala.app if you think someone else has access to your account.

### Your email account

To bring in transactions, you can connect a Gmail or Outlook account that you own or are allowed to use, one at a time. Hasala reads it with read-only access on your iPhone. Connect only a mailbox you're entitled to give Hasala access to. You can disconnect it at any time.

## Suspension

We may suspend an account if we reasonably believe it breaks these terms, puts other users or the service at risk, or if the law requires it. Where it's safe and lawful, we'll tell you why.

As Hasala works today, while your account is suspended you can still:

- open Hasala and see the data already on your iPhone;
- sign in and sign out; and
- delete your account.

You can't sync new changes, connect your email, change your sign-in methods or devices, sign out everywhere, use your recovery phrase to get back in, reset a password or view your recovery phrase. If you think we've made a mistake, email us and we'll review it.

Suspension is something we do. It is different from freezing, which you can do yourself with the Freeze link in a security email if a sign-in wasn't you. The beta app has no freeze button, but you can lift a freeze in the app; see our [Security](/security) page.

## Ending your use of Hasala

### You can leave at any time

You can stop using Hasala and delete your account in the app under Profile › Delete your account. Our server erases your account and sealed data straight away. Encrypted backups expire within 30 days. The [Support](/support) page has step-by-step instructions, including how to leave the TestFlight beta.

Deleting the app from your iPhone does not delete your account.

### We can change or end the beta, or your access

We may end your access if you seriously or repeatedly break these terms. We may also change, pause or end the beta, or close Hasala, at any time. When we do, accounts and their data may be reset or deleted. Where we can, we'll tell you in the app or by email first, so that you can export your data. When an account is closed, its data is deleted from our server as described in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).
